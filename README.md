# Diary of a Lean Bulk Beast — Master 14-Day A/B Workout Journal

A custom **Diary of a Wimpy Kid / Yellow Notebook styled** progressive overload workout logger built for **The Master 14-Day (Week A / Week B) Hypertrophy, Core & Vitality Program**.

## 🏋️ Athlete Profile & Parameters
- **Height / Weight:** 6'3" (190.5 cm) / 77 kg (Lean Bulking Phase)
- **Schedule Structure:** 14-Day Rotating Block (Week A = Days 1 to 7, Week B = Days 8 to 14)
- **Session Duration Cap:** 75 to 85 minutes (Strictly under 90 mins to protect testosterone)
- **Daily Pre-Workout Protocol:** 8–10 mins Zone 2 Incline Treadmill Walking (3.5% incline, 5.2 km/h) for eNOS & synovial warmth
- **Progressive Overload Rule:** Double progression: hold weight until top of rep ceiling is reached at 1–2 RIR across sets, then increase +2.5kg (or 1 pin).

## ✨ Features Added in this Version

1. **100% Authentic Handwritten Typography**:
   - Every title, cue, set, rep, weight, and input uses hand-drawn notebook fonts (`Patrick Hand`, `Kalam`, `Caveat`, `Architects Daughter`).
   - Lined legal pad rulings and red margin lines match the text height.

2. **Notebook Scribble Strike-through Animation**:
   - When you complete all sets of an exercise (or click `✏️ Scribble Done`), an **animated pencil strike-through line slashes across the exercise in red/charcoal ink** with a realistic pen-scratch audio effect!
   - A hand-drawn stamp `[ CROSSED OFF ✓ ]` appears.

3. **Reserved YouTube Shorts Video Slots**:
   - Under every single exercise, there is an expandable `🎬 Technique Video (YouTube Short)` slot.
   - You can paste any YouTube Short link (e.g. `https://youtube.com/shorts/...`), and it embeds into a vertical 9:16 mobile-friendly player and persists in `localStorage`.

4. **Master 14-Day (Week A / Week B) Schedule**:
   - Full 14 days configured:
     - **Week A (Days 1–7):** Upper Body, Lower Body A (BB Hip Thrust), Active Recovery, Push, Pull, Legs B (DB Split Squat + KB Swings/Plank), Rest.
     - **Week B (Days 8–14):** Upper Body, Lower Body A (Leg Press Wide/High), Active Recovery, Push, Pull, Legs B (Adductor/Abductor + Rowing VO2 Max HIIT), Rest.
   - Dynamic 14-day cycle selector and Week A / Week B toggle.

5. **Instant Phone Connect (Public HTTPS Tunnel + QR Code)**:
   - When you launch `run_mobile_server.bat`, it automatically starts a secure HTTPS tunnel via SSH (`https://xxxx.lhr.life`).
   - The app's **"📱 Phone Connect"** button displays a QR code pointing directly to this public HTTPS URL, so ANY phone camera can scan and open it immediately with NO local Wi-Fi firewall issues!

6. **How to get it as a Standalone App or APK**:
   - **One-Tap Standalone App (PWA):**
     - Open the link on your phone.
     - iPhone (Safari): Tap **Share** `⎋` ➔ **"Add to Home Screen"** `➕`.
     - Android (Chrome): Tap **"Install App"** in the top banner.
     - *Result:* Runs 100% full-screen without browser bars, with custom Wimpy Kid home-screen icon, and works completely offline in gym basements!
